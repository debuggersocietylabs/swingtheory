The app

Swing Theory is developed by Debugger Society. We do not collect or transmit personal data from the app. The game works offline and does not include analytics, tracking, an account system, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

Your scores and settings

Your best score and preferences, including sound, haptics, and reduced effects, are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us and do not sync between devices through Swing Theory.

Deleting the app removes its local data. We cannot recover a score that was never sent to us.

Advertising

Swing Theory does not display ads and does not include an advertising SDK.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as a message, screenshots, or device details. We use that information to respond and troubleshoot your request. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

We retain correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to provide support or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing debuggersociety@zohomail.com.

This website

This website does not set its own cookies or include analytics, advertising scripts, or external fonts. It is prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and protect the service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Policy updates

If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. This policy describes Swing Theory version 1.0.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact debuggersociety@zohomail.com.
